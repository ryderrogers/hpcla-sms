Privacy policy.

How Happy To Help LLC collects, uses, and protects information — including mobile phone numbers and SMS-related data.

Information we collect

Happy To Help LLC collects the minimum information necessary to operate the biohacker-os personal health-optimization program. Specifically:

  • Mobile phone number of the program owner (single registered recipient)
  • Biometric data the owner authorizes (heart rate variability, sleep, recovery score, step count) supplied by their own wearable devices
  • Calendar events the owner authorizes the program to read and modify
  • Genetic data the owner has explicitly uploaded for personalized protocol generation
  • Operational logs sufficient to debug and audit the system

How we use it

Information is used exclusively to generate and deliver the personal biohacker-os messages, schedule events on the owner's authorized calendar, and produce daily health protocols. It is not used for any other purpose.

SMS data & non-sharing

Happy To Help LLC does not share, sell, rent, lease, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or message content to any third party for marketing, advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

This non-sharing commitment applies to all information collected as part of the biohacker-os SMS program, including consent records, message logs, and any metadata associated with messages sent or received.

The only third parties that handle SMS data are infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver messages (Twilio for SMS transport, mobile carriers for delivery). These providers process data on Happy To Help LLC's behalf under their own privacy commitments and are not authorized to use the data for any other purpose.

Data security

Credentials, biometric data, and message logs are stored on access-controlled infrastructure and protected with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Access is limited to the program owner and authorized personnel.

Your rights

The program recipient may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data at any time by emailing ryder@hpcla.com. SMS opt-out may be exercised at any time by replying STOP to any message; opt-out is immediate and confirmed by a final acknowledgement message.

Children

The biohacker-os program is not directed at and not available to individuals under 18. Happy To Help LLC does not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the bottom of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated to the program recipient via SMS or email.
